Invoice processing

Extract the invoice, match it, park exceptions for a human. Two-way match if receipts aren't digital yet.

The problem

The invoice arrived. Someone still has to retype it.

AP isn't slow because people are slow. It's slow because the paper (or the PDF) has to become a record by hand.

We install intake that extracts the bill and matches it to what you already have — PO, receipt, or both — and leaves a queue of exceptions a person actually decides.

Controller · AP lead · owner-operator

What we build

Scoped to one outcome

  • Invoice extract into the system you already use.
  • Match against PO and/or receipt. Exceptions sit in a queue — not auto-paid.
  • Playbook for the exception path, so the loop runs without us.
