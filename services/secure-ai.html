Three pictures of ownership

Cloud-native

In your tenancy

Workhorse model as reserved or scale-to-zero GPUs in your GCP, Azure, or AWS account. Default when there's no office box and you already have a cloud bill.

Hybrid · we recommend

On the LAN + in your VPC

Everyday work on hardware that stays in your office, with a batch model in your cloud. Same ownership picture. Two places the work can run.

On-prem

Every cable is yours

Local cluster, local adjudicator, no required egress. For teams who want to show every cable, or have no usable cloud tenancy.

What this is not

Not a wrapper around a lab

A vendor API with the lights off is not private AI.

We will not sell a “private GPT” that still sends your work to a lab. If you later insist on a frontier burst, that’s a written exception with an egress receipt — not the product.

Also not included: a knowledge map, connectors, a morning brief, or “your Drive answers now.” Those ship with the company brain install.
